SpaceSharp is free, open source and complete. Nothing is held back for people who pay, and that will not change. The supporter edition is for those who want to say thanks anyway: a one-time contribution, of an amount you choose, and a supporter key in return.

## What the key does

- **About** shows "Supporter edition" under the version.
- **About › Supporter edition** thanks you by the name you chose.
- **About › Credits** lists the supporters who asked to be named. That list is part of the app, so a new name appears with the release after the contribution.

It unlocks no feature. The app with a key and the app without one do the same things.

## Getting a key

[spacesharp.app/supporter](https://spacesharp.app/supporter/) has the button. Payment is handled by Polar, which is the seller on the receipt and takes care of the tax for your country. The checkout asks, optionally, for a name to show; leave it empty to stay unnamed. The key appears on the page right after the payment and is sent to your email as well.

## Entering it

1. Press **F1** for About and choose **Supporter edition**.
2. Paste the key. It starts with `SSUP1` and is about 200 characters long; it does not matter if the email broke it over several lines.
3. Choose **Use this key**.

**Remove the key from this PC** on the same screen takes it out again. The key is kept in your settings file, and Reset to defaults leaves it where it is. On a new PC, paste the same key from the email; there is no limit on how many of your own PCs it is used on.

## Privacy

The key is a short note (the name you chose, the order number and the date) with a signature. SpaceSharp checks the signature against a public key built into the app, on your PC. Entering a key makes no network request, the app never reports that a key is in use, and a key keeps working offline and does not expire.

What the project keeps on its server for each contribution is the order number, the date, the email address the key was sent to, the name you asked to be shown and the key itself, so a lost key can be sent again. Payment details stay with Polar.
